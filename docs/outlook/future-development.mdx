## Further Mathematical Refinement

The mathematical model can be improved further by using more on-chain data from real sandwich attacks. This can help validate the current assumptions, refine the curves and parameters, and make Pulse Hook better aligned with real-world attack patterns.

## Launchpad Integrations

Pulse Hook is designed to operate independently of external modules. Combined with its Pulse Hook is designed to work independently, making it suitable for launchpads, including smaller teams and emerging networks with limited infrastructure.

Future work can focus on integrations with launchpads of different sizes. [MIT License](https://github.com/no-hive/pulse_hook/blob/main/LICENSE) also makes Pulse Hook easy to adopt and adapt. Developers can use the [mathematical materials](https://pulse-hook.mintlify.site/math/overview) to adapt the Hook's calculations to the characteristics and protection requirements of their own pools.

## Ecosystem Integrations

Pulse Hook should also become more visible across the Uniswap ecosystem. This includes official Uniswap Hook verification and inclusion in relevant Hook lists if eligible.

Community integrations are also underway, including [HookGuard](https://chaosxcode.github.io/hookguard/). The first integration commit is already in place - check it here - [https://github.com/no-hive/pulse\_hook/pull/1](https://github.com/no-hive/pulse_hook/pull/1).

## Independent Security Audit

An independent security audit would be valuable for Pulse Hook. Although the project has undergone an internal review, external assessment is especially important because the Hook includes casts and calculation curves with non-trivial mathematical behavior.

An external audit can provide an additional review of the implementation, mathematical assumptions, edge cases, and potential security risks before broader adoption.
